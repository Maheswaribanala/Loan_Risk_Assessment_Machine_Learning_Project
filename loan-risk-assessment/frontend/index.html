How it works

From application to decision, in one pipeline

1
Enter applicant details

Income, credit history, loan amount, employment, and more — validated on the way in.

2
Feature engineering

Debt-to-income, loan-to-income, repayment capacity, and income stability are derived automatically.

3
ML prediction

The best-performing trained model (selected by ROC-AUC/F1, not accuracy alone) scores the applicant.

4
Explainable result

Risk category, probability, and the leading contributing factors are returned and logged to MySQL.
